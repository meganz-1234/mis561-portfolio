I completed Anthropic's AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations course, which introduced the 4D Framework: Delegation, Description, Discernment, and Diligence. The framework helped me understand that using AI effectively involves more than just writing prompts. It also means knowing which tasks to delegate, giving AI clear instructions, evaluating its responses, and taking responsibility for the final work.

This certificate shows my understanding of how to use AI as a supporting tool while keeping human judgment involved throughout the process.
