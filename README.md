**How I Use AI**

I use generative AI as a supporting tool throughout my analytical workflow. I have used AI to help brainstorm ideas, improve presentation wording, critique dashboard designs, troubleshoot calculations, and communicate business recommendations more clearly.

I evaluate AI suggestions rather than automatically accepting them. I am responsible for checking my calculations, verifying information against the original data, and deciding whether a suggestion actually improves the analysis.
